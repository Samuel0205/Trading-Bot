Questions

The things clients ask first.

Do I own the website?

Yes — completely. The site, the domain, and the content are yours. If you ever leave, we hand everything over. No lock-in, no hostage situations.

What does "from" mean in the pricing?

It's the typical starting point for that tier. The final fixed price depends on how many pages and features you need, and we agree it in writing before any work begins.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. Care plans are optional. You can host and maintain the site yourself — but most owners prefer to hand it off so it stays fast, secure, and updated.

How long does it take?

Most sites launch in two to four weeks once we have your content. Online stores can take a little longer depending on the number of products.

I don't have photos or text. Can you help?

Absolutely. We write the copy for you and can arrange local photography. Many of our clients start with nothing but an idea and a logo.

Will it show up on Google?

Every site is built with SEO best practices, and we set up your Google Business Profile at launch. Ongoing ranking improves fastest with a care plan.
